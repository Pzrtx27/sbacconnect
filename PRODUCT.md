# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students, teachers, and academic staff of Siam Business Administration Technological College (SBAC) Nontaburi, using the app on mobile and web to manage their daily campus life.

## Product Purpose
Sbacconnect serves as a smart campus portal app for tracking gate entry/exit times, wallet balance, calendar, grades, behavior scores, timetable, barista coffee orders, and submitting leaves.

## Brand Personality
Modern, professional, trustful, and vibrant. It represents a technological college, combining deep academic navy blue with energetic red accents in a premium, glassmorphism-enhanced mobile-first wrapper.

## Anti-references
- Cluttered, overwhelming forms with small target sizes.
- Low-contrast text (e.g., light gray text on white background) making it hard for students/teachers to read on-the-go.
- Static, lifeless UI without subtle micro-interactions or transitions.

## Design Principles
1. **Gate to Class Accessibility**: Mobile-first design focusing on high legibility, large tap targets, and clear input fields with proper numeric helpers.
2. **Cohesive Theme Aesthetics**: Strong contrast, clean typography (IBM Plex Sans Thai), and elegant dark/light theme switching.
3. **Realistic & Solid States**: Clean loading indicators, micro-animations using Framer Motion, and clear feedback toasts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High color contrast meeting WCAG AA standards.
- Proper keyboard inputs (`inputMode="numeric"` for numerical fields like ID and National ID).
- Responsive layout scaling cleanly down to small mobile viewports.
